## Product Hunt

**Tagline:** Multi-Agent AI Camera — 7 Edge AI detectors + XiaoZhi AI + ESP-Claw on ESP32-S3

**Description:**
NanoCam is an open-source ESP32-S3 AI camera that runs three AI engines on the same hardware:
- Mode A: 7 offline AI detectors (cat face, human face, color, QR code, etc.)
- Mode B: XiaoZhi AI — LLM voice chatbot with visual understanding
- Mode C: ESP-Claw — Chat-as-Creation (IM + Lua, zero-code device control)

Control via serial AT commands. Works with Arduino, Python, STM32, Micro:bit. 28-chapter tutorial included.
